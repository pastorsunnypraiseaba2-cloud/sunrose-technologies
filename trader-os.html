Key features

A complete analysis workspace

Every feature serves one purpose: helping you read the market clearly and follow your plan with discipline.

Sunrose Vision

Upload your Daily, 4-Hour and 1-Hour charts for a structured, multi-timeframe analysis with clear entry zones, invalidation and targets.

CPR and key levels

Monthly, weekly and daily Central Pivot Range levels, plus Camarilla levels, mapped automatically so you see the market's key decision areas.

Institutional market structure

Trend, breaks of structure and changes of character framed so you always know which side of the market you are on.

Liquidity analysis

Highlights where liquidity rests and where it has been taken, helping you avoid common traps.

Trend continuation methodology

Built around a written trading constitution focused on continuation setups, so every decision follows the same rules.

Risk management support

Defined invalidation and targets listed nearest first, helping you plan risk before you enter.

Sunrose Forex Academy

Built-in lessons and guides that teach you to analyse the market yourself, not just follow signals.

Alerts and dashboard

A clean member dashboard on web and Android with timely alerts.

No. It is an analysis and education tool. All trading decisions, and their results, remain yours.

No. Sunrose Trader OS analyses markets and supports your decision-making. It does not execute trades on your account.

Which markets does it support?

It is designed for currency pairs and suits both swing and intraday traders.

Which platforms can I use?

Sunrose Trader OS runs in any modern web browser, with an Android app planned for Google Play.

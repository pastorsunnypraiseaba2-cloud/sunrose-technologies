About Sunrose Technologies

A technology company building intelligent solutions for a global future.

Sunrose Technologies Limited exists to turn ideas and real-world problems into practical, intelligent technology that people and organisations can rely on.

Who we are

Engineers, product builders and problem solvers

Sunrose Technologies is a software and artificial intelligence company. We design, develop and operate our own digital products, and we build custom solutions for individuals, businesses, churches and institutions.

Our work brings together software engineering, applied AI and a disciplined, engineering-minded approach to solving problems. We are the technology home of the SUNROSE brand.

What we do

We build custom software, web and mobile applications, AI-powered tools and automated workflows, and we develop our own products such as Sunrose Trader OS and Shepherd OS.

Why we exist

Too many good ideas and organisations are held back by tools that are expensive, complicated or built for someone else. We exist to make intelligent technology practical and accessible.

Our philosophy of innovation

Innovation is only valuable when it solves a real problem. We start with the problem, build the simplest thing that works, then refine it with real users.

Our commitment

Solving real problems through technology

We build products we use ourselves, so we feel every rough edge and fix it. We are clear about what software can and cannot do, and we never promise outcomes technology cannot guarantee.

Our global ambition

Built in Nigeria, designed for the world

Our long-term ambition is to become a globally recognised technology company, serving customers across Africa, the United Kingdom, North America, Europe and beyond.

Mission

To design and deliver innovative, intelligent and accessible technology solutions that solve real-world problems, empower businesses and improve the way people live and work.

Vision

To become a globally recognised technology company distinguished by innovation, excellence, integrity and the development of transformative digital solutions.
